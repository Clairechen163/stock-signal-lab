## Does deciding before the open help?

From 2022-02-10 to 2026-11-12 (239 weekly decisions, both traded at the open), labels set with the pre-market price returned +34.9% a year (worst drawdown -34.2%) against +35.3% (-34.1%) for labels set at the previous close; the two agreed on 89% of stock-weeks. Sharpe ratio difference -0.02 (95% block-bootstrap CI [-0.20, +0.17]): deciding before the open has been **not distinguishable from deciding on the previous close**. *Stability: same verdict in 9 of the last 9 runs.*

## Do opening gaps carry into the day?

Across 19,026 stock-days (18 watchlist stocks, 2021-11-18 to 2026-11-16), each 1% of opening gap went with a -0.03% move from the open to the close (95% block-bootstrap CI [-0.08%, +0.03%]): no reliable relationship, so the size of a gap has not told you which way the session goes. *Stability: same verdict in 9 of the last 9 runs.*
